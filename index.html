SUNTANU DAS — THE MUSICIAN BEHIND THE TEACHER

Suntanu Das is a professional musician and music teacher with over 25 years of experience in the music industry.

Having studied guitar with the legendary guitarist Paul Gilbert, Suntanu brings world-class techniques, tone control, and deep musicality to his teaching. He is a Trinity Grade 8 Rock & Pop Guitarist and is currently serving as a Music Teacher at GD Goenka Public School.

Beyond being an accomplished guitarist, Suntanu is proficient in Bass Guitar and Piano/Keyboard. His expertise extends into sound engineering, music production, composition, recording, and mixing, utilizing FL Studio as his primary DAW.

TRINITY ROCK & POP CERTIFICATION

Structured Grade 1 through Grade 8 curriculum guidance with a certified Grade 8 guitarist

Trinity College London Rock & Pop exams measure real-world performance skills. Suntanu provides complete coaching for song selection, technique, playback, improvisation, and exam confidence.

Grades 1 – 2

Foundation

Basic hand positioning, single-note riffs, fundamental rhythm chords, and introductory sight reading.

Grades 3 – 4

Development

Syncopated rhythms, power chords, minor pentatonic scales, tone management, and soloing foundations.

Grades 5 – 6

Intermediate Mastery

Complex time signatures, modal improvisation, string bending, vibrato expression, and dynamic phrasing.

Grades 7 – 8

Virtuosic Performance

Complete professional repertoire, advanced alternate picking, legato mastery, and Grade 8 distinction prep.
