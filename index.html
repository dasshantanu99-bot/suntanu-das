THE MUSICIAN BEHIND THE TEACHER

Suntanu Das is a professional musician and music teacher with over 25 years of experience in the music industry.

Having studied guitar with the legendary guitarist Paul Gilbert, Suntanu brings world-class techniques and musicality to his teaching. He is a Trinity Grade 8 Rock & Pop Guitarist and is currently serving as a Music Teacher at GD Goenka Public School.

Beyond being an accomplished guitarist, Suntanu is proficient in Bass Guitar and Piano/Keyboard. His expertise extends into sound engineering, music production, composition, recording, and mixing, utilizing FL Studio as his primary DAW.
